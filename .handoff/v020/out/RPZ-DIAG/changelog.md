### Added

- RPZ feed diagnostics on both store drivers: `kudurru lists rpz [name]` and the `GET /api/v1/blocklists/_rpz` and `GET /api/v1/blocklists/{namespace}/{name}/rpz` routes show each feed's state on the answering node (pending, serving, expired or unavailable; clients whose policy selects a feed that is not serving get SERVFAIL), serial, primary, last confirmation, expiry, next attempt, last error, rule counts by trigger and action, and every ignored rule reason with its count and example owners. `kudurru doctor` and `/info/messages` report feeds that are not serving cleanly or ignore rules, and `/metrics` adds `kudurru_rpz_feed_available`, `kudurru_rpz_feed_expires_timestamp_seconds`, `kudurru_rpz_feed_confirmed_timestamp_seconds`, `kudurru_rpz_feed_serial`, `kudurru_rpz_feed_rules` and `kudurru_rpz_feed_ignored_rules`.
- An RPZ deploy guide (`docs/deploy/rpz.md`) listing the supported triggers and actions, which policy is ignored and counted, the evaluation precedence, and how to check a feed on each node.
