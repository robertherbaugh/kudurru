| D404 | Primary parent and child zones on one node | (1) Cause of the SERVFAIL until a restart (`zone awaiting committed publication`) of a primary child under a primary parent: a cycle of two exact checks. Every committed-status write or edit advances a `DNSZone`'s resourceVersion, so a lifecycle receipt is often behind its `DNSZone` until the driver realigns it. Fencing a child in its parent and the child's transition that records that obligation required the exact receipt of both zones, in the SQLite adapter (`source`) and again in `currentguard.checkBackground`, while realigning either receipt is itself a lifecycle CAS whose membership check needs that fence. (2) One rule, in one place: the SQLite adapter decides which commands change only a parent/child boundary (the `parent-fence`, `late-parent-fence` and `parent-release` admissions with a parent, `boundaryCommand`; a lifecycle transition that changes nothing but `Sequence` and `Parents`, `boundaryTransition`) and reads their sources with `boundarySource`: the exact receipt still qualifies, and otherwise the same zone (`sqliteadapter.SameBoundaryZone`: object name, UID of the lifecycle and of its source, type, apex, a `DNSZone` not being deleted, a source not removed). It marks the production guard's check `BackgroundCheck.Boundary`; the guard applies the same `SameBoundaryZone` rule only for that flag with the `lifecycle` reason or a boundary reason, refuses the flag for any other reason, and keeps the exact check for every unmarked check. A boundary command never advances a receipt; realignment stays exact (the driver's Preparing-to-Fencing CAS, a primary `Reconcile`, a nonprimary refresh, and `Runtime.FenceParents`' own Preparing-to-Fencing CAS, which now carries the source observed at that moment as the driver's does). This replaces the CORE-OPEN4 variant (D385 item 4), which relaxed the guard by reason alone and made the fence's child transition record the child's current source. (3) DNS across the pair's own transitions: a parent with a desired child that is not yet fenced keeps serving its committed head and is not admitted (UPDATE, NOTIFY, status and publication acknowledgment wait, as D395 does for a zone's own write); a child whose obligation is witnessed by a parent that is still Fencing keeps serving and stays admitted. (4) A new child: until its first publication, names at and below its apex answer SERVFAIL from its unavailable coverage, not the parent's data or a referral. The driver steps a zone that is being created ahead of its fair rotation (a desired zone without an active lifecycle of its own source, not backing off, without moving the rotation's cursor), and a step that committed a lifecycle change needing another step (creation, a parent fence, the Preparing-to-Fencing CAS, the source refresh of a nonprimary zone that is not yet active, and for a re-created zone the release or retirement of its old incarnation) ends the pass so the next pass resumes at that zone, at most eight times in a row; the refresh of an active nonprimary zone only wakes the loop, since it repeats on every change of its `DNSZone`. Every step keeps its own admission and publication wait; only the order changes. Measured on a mounted node without the race detector: with three zones the first answer came 0.5 to 5.2 s after the child's POST (up to about 5 s at load 20) (0.9 to 2.5 s before, so the order hardly matters there); with 23 zones, in alternating runs, 2.5 to 4.9 s (median 3.9 s) against 3.4 to 14.6 s (median 4.5 s) before. The mounted test bounds it at 15 s without the race detector. The D395 bound (about 0.3 s) is not reached: the child's own four or five steps, each a transaction, a publication wait and a fresh read, and the rest of the pass already running when it was created remain. | A deployment with `example.` and `child.example.` as primaries on one node is ordinary, and an authoritative zone that stays SERVFAIL until a restart is a release defect. The boundary commands neither read nor record a zone's configuration (`candidate` already allows only the one child reservation to change), so the exact resourceVersion and intent add no safety there and only couple them to the status writer; zone identity stays exact and a deleting zone is refused, the stricter of the two lanes' rules. Not advancing a receipt in a boundary command keeps one meaning for the receipt (the source the zone's head and lifecycle were last checked against), so no source change can be recorded without the exact path that rebuilds from it. A flag set by the adapter, which knows the command, is narrower than a reason the guard cannot tell apart from other uses. A negative answer from the parent for names under the new apex would be cached by resolvers for the parent's negative TTL and hide the child for minutes after it activates; SERVFAIL is retried within seconds. Before the scheduling change each of a new zone's three or four steps waited behind a reconcile and publication wait of every other zone, so the window grew with the number of zones. | Keeping both lanes' relaxations (rejected: two relaxed paths, one by reason in the guard and one by flag); recording the child's current source in the fence transition (rejected: a boundary command would advance the receipt, including a new intent digest, outside the exact realignment, and the out-of-transaction read races the next status write); making the driver realign receipts before fencing (rejected: the realignment CAS needs the fence); suppressing status writes while a fence is pending (rejected: couples the status writer to driver internals); answering names under a new child apex from the parent until the child is admitted (rejected: negative caching for the parent's negative TTL); a synthesized referral (rejected: the parent has no delegation data and the referral leads back to the same unavailable zone on this node); skipping the publication wait of unchanged acknowledged zones (rejected: changes the acknowledgment rule, and the ordering change already removes those waits from a new zone's path); ending a running pass on every store change so a new zone leads sooner (tried and reverted: one full read per step under constant change, and the driver tests under race went from 142 s to 527 s with three failures); resetting a parent's retry backoff from its children (measured by CORE-CHILD-ZONE and reverted). |
