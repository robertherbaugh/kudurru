| D371 | Query-log delivery to additional sinks (V2-OBS contract 6) | `querylog.sinks` (hot cluster setting, at most 8) adds destinations beside the primary `querylog.sink`. The one collector applies exclusions and the privacy mode once, stamps the ID, publishes to the tail, then offers the same immutable event without waiting to one bounded queue per sink (default 10,000; overflow is dropped for that sink and counted in `kudurru_querylog_dropped_total{stage="sink",sink}`). Each sink has its own goroutine, batch policy, attempt timeout and attempt budget; a batch is rewritten only after an error the sink marks as not applied (`querylog.Retryable`), otherwise dropped, and never replayed or moved. The privacy transform thus runs before every sink queue; `nothing` offers no per-query event and events carry no wire data (raw dnstap stays a separate audited override). Sinks follow D266: each prepared route holds a reference per sink (bundle release hook on SQLite, the reload subscriber on Kubernetes); an unchanged entry keeps its running sink; a new one is opened by its first event from its own goroutine, with backoff, and never backfilled; an unreferenced one retires behind the events already queued, drains at most five seconds and closes. Where requests do not hold their route (the reload subscriber of the Kubernetes driver), a query that read the replaced route just before the swap can reach a sink that has already retired: it is never handed to the entry's new sink, because a route's privacy mode and its sinks are one captured decision, and it is counted as dropped while the name is still configured; for a removed name nothing was owed and nothing is counted. At shutdown the sinks share the collector's five-second bound; a sink that did not drain is logged by name, apart from the local flush. ClickHouse and Loki ship as direct HTTP batch sinks on this host with the standard library client; dnstap to DNS-collector stays the documented path for other destinations and wire-level consumers. | A slow exporter must not stall DNS, local SQLite persistence or live tails, and the collector had one queue and one destination. Direct ClickHouse/Loki sinks are required v0.2 scope, need no second process and export exactly what the privacy mode keeps; an SDK would threaten the binary budget (D138, D260). | Fan-out inside the primary batch flush; one shared exporter queue; blocking or unbounded queues; blind host-side retry of uncertain writes; opening sinks during preparation (an unreachable exporter would fail startup or a publication); backfilling from the tail or `querylog.db`; restarting every sink on reload; retiring a removed sink at adoption (held requests and queued events would miss it); handing a replaced route's in-flight query to the entry's new sink, or dropping it uncounted; reporting an exporter that did not drain at shutdown as an incomplete local flush; exporting under `nothing`; DNS-collector as the only route to ClickHouse and Loki; vendor SDKs |
