kudurru's core uses the Kudurru Community License for personal, homelab,
qualifying nonprofit and community-development uses. Business and government
production use needs an accepted commercial agreement and Order Form
(see [Licensing](/project/licensing/) and the [license text](/project/license/);
[NOTICE](https://github.com/robertherbaugh/kudurru/blob/main/NOTICE) lists the third-party notices;
[kudurru.io/license](https://kudurru.io/license) is where to request a key).
dnsdist and keepalived are GPL-2.0 and are distributed only as separate,
unmodified programs in their own images.

Anyone may read and link to these docs. Their source has a separate
documentation notice; the marketing website and license server have
source-viewing licenses that permit inspection, with reuse requiring
prior written authorization.
